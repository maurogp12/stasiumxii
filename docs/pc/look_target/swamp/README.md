# Swamp (Gloomfen / West Southbridge swamp): Mauro's reference (16 Oct 2026)

`mauro_swamp_reference.mp4` is a 50 s pan, 960×540. `mauro_swamp_reference_sheet.jpg` holds frames sampled every 4 s. Mauro: "este se supone sea el pantano", meaning this is what the swamp is supposed to be.

The look:

**Layout**
- Mossy grass islands joined by plank boardwalks over dark, still water.
- Lily pads and reeds in the water.

**Landmarks**
- A witch's hut with a thatched roof, a bubbling green cauldron, and purple and red mushrooms.
- Stone ruins around a glowing green pool.
- Thatched stilt huts with ladders.
- A giant purple mushroom on one island.

**The dark parts**
- A bare, twisted dead tree on a muddy island.
- Bones, skulls and a wrecked rowing boat.

**Atmosphere**
- Cypress trees standing in the water.
- Rolling fog banks.
- A muted teal and olive palette, with green glow as the accent.
- A dirt road and signpost at the edge, where the swamp joins the land.

Everything is painted, at the same iso angle as the world.
