# Southbridge: Mauro's reference (16 Oct 2026)

`mauro_southbridge_reference.mp4` is a 40 s pan, 960×540. `mauro_southbridge_reference_sheet.jpg` holds frames sampled every 4 s. Mauro: "se supone que esté así", meaning this is how Southbridge is supposed to look.

The look:

**Land**
- Lush green farmland, dense with weeping willows and autumn trees (orange, red and gold).
- A winding blue stream crossed by a small stone bridge.
- Flower clumps and rocks.

**Roads**
- A wide dirt road with signposts.
- Smaller dirt paths branching to the farms.

**Buildings**
- A red barn, a thatched farmhouse, a market stall, a windmill, a blue-roof cottage, and a corral with a stable and hay.

**Life**
- Villagers walking, a hay cart and animals.

**Day and night**
- A full day-to-night cycle: at night there are lit windows, street lamps and fireflies.

The current state is the in-game Southbridge on `pc/world-seamless`. A capture follows for comparison.
