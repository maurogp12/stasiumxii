This page is built from the files under `docs/pc/art_help/`, from #252 comments by Claude (GitHub user `maurogp12`, trailer `Generated by Claude Code`) and by cursor, and from the v2 blockout README on `claude/class-walk-blockouts` at `b1a1cff98ce8e68328d04b2a07af0f95a9c8b831`. Each decision cites a commit or a comment. Where the record does not say, the cell is **open**.

## 1. Purpose and scope

PC only. These are 2D painted walk cycles (12 frames, combat facings), kept as reference so Scenario Art, Claude, and later teams can match look and motion. They are not game sprites and they are not wired into the client.

PR #252 is docs only and stays a draft. It is never merged. Do not touch `mobile` or `main`. The PC change log states the same limit: never merged into `main` or `mobile` by an agent; only Mauro merges (`docs/pc/CHANGE_LOG_PC.md`).

| Mauro and Luca | Approve looks, lock a version, or park a leftover. |

## 3. Locked rules

These are the rules later packages are built to. Where two comments disagree, both are cited and the gap is **open**.

1. **Head and eyes face the travel direction.** Head yaw follows chest yaw, and the difference stays within 10 degrees. [5979810395](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5979810395). Kestrel's brief repeats that the head faces travel (S down-right, E up-right): [5981085130](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5981085130).
2. **Files use the combat letters S, W, N, E.** No remap from older art letters. [5979810395](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5979810395).
3. **W is a mirror of S, and N is a mirror of E, for every class.** Hand swaps are accepted: Bastion's shield and Kestrel's bow change hands in W and N. No separate W/N paint. [5980461396](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5980461396).
4. **Rig on the v2 blockout joints only.** Hip height as a fraction of height, from the v2 README at `b1a1cff9`: Bastion 0.60, Kestrel 0.62, Gloam 0.61, Mender 0.60. Same table in [5980139068](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5980139068). The blockouts are not on this branch. They live on `claude/class-walk-blockouts` at `b1a1cff98ce8e68328d04b2a07af0f95a9c8b831`, under `docs/pc/art_help/class_walk_blockouts/`.
5. **Leg pieces are cut from the target at upper-body scale.** At most 10% stretch along the bone, and none across it. [5980830379](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5980830379). Bastion v3 scores record the same cap (`bastion/v3/scores.md` at `7f65035e`: along-bone stretch S max 1.086, E cap 1.10).
6. **Legs are rooted at the target's own hip and belt points, plus the pelvis delta.** The blockout supplies rotation and foot plants, not the hip position. [5980830379](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5980830379) and `bastion/v3/scores.md`.
7. **Checks before Mauro sees a walk.**
   - Greave width / shoulder width within 8% of the target. [5980830379](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5980830379).
   - Hip midpoint within 4 px of the belt centre at the contact frames. [5980830379](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5980830379).
   - Belt-to-sole on the straight leg within 5% of the target. The straight leg at contact is the one that is compared; a bent weight-bearing leg can sit shorter. [5980813208](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5980813208).
   - Look 87 or higher, with motion 100, is the bar Bastion v3 was held to (`scores.md`: "S look margin: 87.1, with the 87 limit"; motion 100 in `metric_S.json` and `metric_E.json`). Claude's re-score is S 87.0 / E 88.7, motion 100, and it says to keep the f06 heel strike so motion stays 100 ([5981013608](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5981013608)). That comment's "same rules" for Kestrel are target-scale legs, target hips, and a swing-lift check. They do not restate 87 or 100. The Kestrel fix brief writes the target as look ≥ 87, motion ≥ 95, qa 48/48 ([5981085130](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5981085130)). Whether motion 100 replaces 95 for every class: **open**. The older written acceptance is look ≥ 85 and motion ≥ 95 (`bastion/v1/scores.md` at `ad15e089`).
8. **Keep the heel-strike frame unchanged if lowering it costs motion.** On Bastion S, f06 is the heel strike. A 6–8 px drop there fails the frame and motion falls to 91.7. [5981013608](https://github.com/maurogp12/stasiumxii/pull/252#issuecomment-5981013608) and `bastion/v3/scores.md`.

Cell format, from the v2 blockout README (`b1a1cff9`): 512×360, pivot (256, 329), 12 frames at 17.144 fps. S is front 3/4 down-right. E is back 3/4 up-right. Contacts: S f00 and f06, E f05 and f11.

### Re-run the score

Committed `metric_*.json` and `qa_walk.json` are Scenario Art's scores. Claude re-runs before Mauro sees a walk.

`bastion_metric.py` (v3 copy) arguments: `--facing`, `--cand`, `--v2`, `--target`, `--joints`, `--idle`, `--target_alpha`, optional `--out`, `--png`, `--prefix` (default `bastion`), `--selftest`. Joints are read from `facings` / `walk_{F}`.

`qa_walk.py` usage, from its header: `qa_walk.py [FRAMES_DIR] [OUT_JSON]`. W and N are `np.fliplr` mirrors.

Joints and clay are on `claude/class-walk-blockouts` @ `b1a1cff9`, not in this branch. Several v3 build scripts still point at `/workspace/handoff/class_walk_blockouts/...` (for example `build.py` and `build_hybrid.py`). A from-scratch rebuild on a clean checkout of this branch alone: **open**.

## 6. How to add or modify a class walk

1. Confirm the look target is one Mauro (and Luca, when he has weighed in) already approved. Do not start from `targets/_orig/`.
2. Cut masks the metric can read (L mode, white figure above 127).
3. Rig only on that class's v2 joints (`b1a1cff9`). Use the hip heights in section 3.4.
4. Apply the locked rules in section 3 from the start: travel-facing head, S/W/N/E filenames, mirrors, target-scale legs rooted at the target hips, and the greave, hip, belt-to-sole, look, and motion checks.
5. Put the package at `docs/pc/art_help/class_walk_looks/<class>/vN/` with frames, contact compares, `scores.md`, metric JSON, and `qa_walk.json`.
6. In that same commit, update this README (status row and a history entry) and add one line to `docs/pc/CHANGE_LOG_PC.md`.
7. Push `art/ironjaw-walk-help` only. Comment on #252. Do not open a new branch or a new PR. Do not merge. Do not touch `mobile` or `main`.
8. Claude re-scores and specs. Mauro and Luca decide whether it locks. Park leftovers in this table instead of folding them into the next class.
