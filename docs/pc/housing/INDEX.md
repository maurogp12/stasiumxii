# Housing art index

Nothing here is wired in Godot or committed to any branch. "IN-GAME-READY cutout" means a transparent PNG that could be placed; it is not hooked up anywhere.

Naming: `<room>_<tier>_<item>.png` (furniture), `<room>_<tier>.jpg` (room concept), `<room>_<tier>_empty.*` (room shell), `house_<tier>[_ext][_half].png` (exterior), `res_<name>_{icon_64,icon_128,world}.png` (resources), `farm_*/pet_*` (animals). Rooms: bedroom=Recámara, kitchen=Cocina, sala=Sala, cellar=Sótano, garden=Jardín.
The 14 failed room shells in `pieces/shells/_wip_autofill_FAILED/` were left out on purpose.
