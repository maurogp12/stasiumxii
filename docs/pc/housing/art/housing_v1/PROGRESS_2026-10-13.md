## 1. House tiers (Luca rule)
| House tier | Rooms |
|---|---|
| Normal | Bedroom only |
| Rare | Bedroom + Living + Kitchen |
| Legendary | All 5: Bedroom, Kitchen, Living, Basement, Garden |

- The **house** and **each room** upgrade in place, in order: Normal → Rare → Legendary. The player pays only the resource difference (Luca rule).
- When a house tier unlocks, its new rooms open **empty at Normal room tier**. Room tiers are independent of the house tier.
- Placed furniture stays where it is; chests keep their contents.
- Where it is documented:
  - `ROOM_TIERS.md`: sections "Tiers de casa" and "Remodelación / Upgrade".
  - `RECIPES.md`: section "Tiers de casa".
  - `HOUSE_TIERS_1280x720.png` and `ROOM_UPGRADES_1280x720.png`.
- House upgrade costs:
  - Normal → Rare: ~x3 total, using mid-region rares plus 2–3★ drops.
  - Rare → Legendary: ~x8 total, using far-region rares plus 4–5★ drops and an Elder bark (WB-5).
