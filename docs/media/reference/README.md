`wakfu_goal_walk.mp4` (a small cut of a Wakfu MMORPG clip Mauro recorded) and

What to take from it:

  never stop between tiles; the camera follows the walker smoothly.
- Floating islands: grass and stone ground as one painted surface (no grid
  while exploring), cliff sides under the edges, clouds drifting below and
  around, deep teal sky.
- Soft bright light, glowing crystals / portals, light shafts, small living
  details (grass, particles).
- Small, clean characters against a big world.
