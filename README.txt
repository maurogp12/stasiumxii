STASIUM XII - PC test build (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)
Build: 10 Oct 2026 (b), pc/world-zones 9467e3f.
New: two more dungeons - the Frostspire Archive (Northgate) and the Saltmaw Grotto (Eastmarch).

1. Download ALL 5 zips and unzip them into the SAME folder (a new folder, or over the old build):
   StasiumXII_PC_2026-10-10b_part1_game.zip
   StasiumXII_PC_2026-10-10b_part2_data.zip
   StasiumXII_PC_2026-10-10b_part3_data.zip
   StasiumXII_PC_2026-10-10b_part4_data.zip
   StasiumXII_PC_2026-10-10b_part5_data.zip
2. Double-click JOIN_AND_PLAY.bat. It joins the game data into StasiumXII.pck, then starts the game.
   After that you can just run StasiumXII.exe.
   SmartScreen: More info -> Run anyway.

Dungeons (click the door or the Door Keeper, pick a star, Enter):
- Old Granary Cellar: Stoneford (west of Crossroads), levels 1-10.
- Frostspire Archive: Northgate (north, the snow town), levels 10-20.
- Saltmaw Grotto: Eastmarch (east coast), sea cave on the beach, levels 20-30.
Hover a monster to see hit % and damage; gold ring = in range.
Controls: click to walk, Shift+click or double-click to run, R toggles run, Esc for Visuals (Performance mode).
